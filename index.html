HERITAGE

A meeting that shaped motoring

Charles Rolls and Henry Royce met in Manchester on 4 May 1904. Their shared ambition brought engineering precision and entrepreneurial vision together under the Rolls-Royce name.

1904

Charles Rolls and Henry Royce meet in Manchester, forging an alliance built on uncompromising standards.

2003

The modern Home of Rolls-Royce begins operations at Goodwood, West Sussex, uniting design and hand craftsmanship.

PRESENT

Craftsmanship, personalization and advanced engineering continue to shape every bespoke commission worldwide.

GOODWOOD

The home of Rolls-Royce

The modern Home of Rolls-Royce opened in Goodwood, West Sussex, on 1 January 2003. The facility brings design, engineering and hand craftsmanship together in one carefully considered environment.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

Crafted with purpose

Each motor car is shaped through a combination of advanced engineering and detailed craftsmanship. Materials, finishes and individual details are considered as part of one complete experience.

BESPOKE

No two visions are the same

Rolls-Royce Bespoke allows clients to personalise colours, materials, embroidery, veneers and carefully selected details. The result is a motor car shaped around an individual vision.

SPIRIT OF ECSTASY

An enduring symbol

The Spirit of Ecstasy has become one of the most recognisable symbols in motoring, representing movement, grace and the heritage of Rolls-Royce.
